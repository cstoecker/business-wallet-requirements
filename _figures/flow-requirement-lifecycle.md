A clause in a law, standard or ecosystem specification is extracted as an obligation, derived into a technology-neutral requirement and reviewed by a named person. Rejected requirements go back for rework; approved ones are published with an immutable EBW-CAT-NNN identifier. Published requirements guide implementers and configure the conformance test cases that implementations are tested against."
