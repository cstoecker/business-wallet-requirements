alt: "Concept model of the trust plane in three bands. Oversight: conformity assessment body and supervisory body. Providers and lists: qualified trust service provider, Member State trusted list and the Commission list of trusted entities. Use: credential, status information and relying party. The conformity assessment body audits the provider, the supervisory body grants qualified status and informs the list, the list lists the provider, the provider issues credentials that have status information, and the relying party validates against both lists and checks status."
caption: "Who establishes trust (oversight, providers and lists) and who uses it (credentials, status and relying parties)."
description: "Concept model of the trust plane for European digital identity and business wallets. A conformity assessment body audits a qualified trust service provider; the supervisory body grants qualified status and informs the body that maintains the Member State trusted list, which then lists the provider. Providers issue credentials that carry status information. A relying party validates against the trusted list and the Commission list of trusted entities, and checks status. Based on Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 as amended, ETSI TS 119 612, ETSI TS 119 602 and the EUDI ARF trust model."
