# 07 — GitHub Discussions: setup and first post

A repository admin must enable Discussions (Settings, General, Features, Discussions). The assistant has no GitHub tool that creates or enables discussions, so this step is manual. The site already links to `https://github.com/spherity/business-wallet-requirements/discussions` from the header, the side navigation and the footer.

Suggested categories: **Announcements** (maintainers only), **Q&A** (answerable questions about concepts and requirements), **Concept feedback** (one thread per concept article), **Sources and standards** (suggest or challenge a source), **Ecosystems and use cases**, **Ideas**.

## First post (category Announcements)

**Title:** Welcome: help us check the European Business Wallet requirements

**Body:**
This site collects requirements for European Business Wallets and trust infrastructure and traces each one to a law, a standard or an ecosystem specification. It is a draft for review. The European Business Wallet is still a Commission proposal (COM(2025) 838), so details can change.

What we would like from you:
- Read a concept article and tell us what is unclear, wrong or missing (one thread per concept in "Concept feedback").
- Suggest official sources or standards we have not registered ("Sources and standards").
- Tell us how your ecosystem or sector uses organisation identity ("Ecosystems and use cases").

Ground rules: we only use official sources, standards, ecosystem specifications and the Open Identity Summit 2025 paper as references. Legal content is analysis, not legal advice. For private questions write to info@spherity.com.
