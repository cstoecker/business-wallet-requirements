# Legal compliance map: access-controlled verifiable evidence graph operated by or with a European Business Wallet (EU and Germany)

Status date: 2026-10-04. Primary sources only: EU texts via publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX> (Accept: application/xhtml+xml, Accept-Language: eng); German federal law via gesetze-im-internet.de (XML export, text as consolidated on the retrieval day); BSI TR from bsi.bund.de. No commentary, no law-firm pages.

Method notes and limits
- "Read in full" = every article cited in the row was read in the text I retrieved this session. "Read in part" = only the cited articles or paragraphs; the rest of the act was not read.
- Rows marked (prior note) rest on the repo notes docs/research/legal-status-check.md, company-law-research.md, ebw-legislative-file-research.md or evidence-claims-3-scale-law-policy.md, which record full or part reads of primary text. I did not re-open them; they are labelled so.
- The EU consolidated eIDAS text (02014R0910-20241018) carries "no legal effect" per its own header. Corrigenda R(10)-R(12) to 910/2014 and R(01)-R(06) to 2024/1183 were not read (prior note).
- Graph element key: identifier, statement, authority, policy, status, log, scoring. "Writer" = party authenticated to add or change graph content; "reader" = party authorised to see it.
- Existing repo coverage: category LEG has 80 requirements (EBW-LEG-001 to 080): NIS2 (001, 021-028), eIDAS legal effects (002-004, 049-054), GDPR (005, 007-020, 066-068, 073-074, 076), DORA (029-031), CRA (032-039, 079-080), AI Act (040-044, 048), digital EU power of attorney (045-047, 055-056), accessibility (057-064), access-control presumption (065), key custody (069-070). Not covered by any LEG row: Data Act, ESPR/DPP, eIDAS Arts 24(2)(h)/42/45h-45l, all German law. Rows below flag "no LEG row" where I saw none by statement text (I read statement lines only, not full files).

## 3. Where Germany adds to the EU baseline

1. Supervision split: BNetzA (signatures, seals, time stamps, delivery, preservation) and BSI (website authentication, NIS2 side) under VDG § 2; the EBW proposal reuses the eIDAS Art 46a supervisory body (B5), so the German EBW supervisor would be BNetzA unless Germany designates otherwise (inference, not a read statement).
2. QTSP detail beyond eIDAS: minimum liability cover EUR 250,000 (VDG § 10); documented, 12-month-retained disclosures of user data to authorities (§ 8(3)); consent and confirmation rules for representation attributes in certificates, with a specific allowance for representation relationships in seal certificates (§ 12(3)); revocation also on request of the represented third party (§ 14(2)); duty to re-protect signed data (§ 15); termination plan with takeover by BNetzA infrastructure (§ 16).
3. Retention: HGB § 257 and AO § 147 give 10, 8 and 6 year periods, with machine readability and availability conditions. eIDAS sets no number; the longer of the periods applies to evidence in the graph that is a book, voucher or business letter.
4. Identifiers: the German register number plus court code (UBRegG § 3(3)) and the W-IdNr (AO § 139c), against the EU EUID. No source read ties them together for the EBW.
5. Scoring: BDSG § 31 adds conditions on scoring of natural persons that go beyond GDPR Art 22; relevant only where scored entities are sole traders or individuals.
6. Cyber: NIS2 is transposed in the BSIG 2025, which names QTSPs as "besonders wichtige Einrichtungen" and keeps the 24 h and 72 h report steps.
7. Supply chain: LkSG adds a 7-year documentation duty for enterprises with 1,000 or more employees in Germany, relevant to graph statements about suppliers.
8. Not found in force: no German law on AI Act, Data Act, CRA or wallet implementation on the site; ÖkodesignG exists but its substantive sections are future-dated; German adaptation of VDG to 2024/1183 looks incomplete in the text read.
