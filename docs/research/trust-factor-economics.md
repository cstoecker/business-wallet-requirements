## D. Framing recommendation

1. Qualitative framing ("trust as a transaction-cost reducer and enabler of exchange") can rest on Arrow 1972, Coase 1937, Williamson 1979 and Akerlof 1970 once the primary texts are opened; today only bibliographic data is confirmed (Arrow additionally by a quoted secondary).
2. The only quantified items with a primary text read are official (SWD 837 figures) and, at abstract level, Dyer and Chu. Keep them in separate claim classes.
3. "Trust as a production factor" is an interpretive label. None of the verified texts uses that phrase; Arrow and Coase support "input to exchange", not "factor of production". State it as the site's framing with cited support, not as a literature finding.
4. A share of verification/compliance cost in B2B or supply-chain transaction cost: **not found**. Say so in the business case rather than estimating.
