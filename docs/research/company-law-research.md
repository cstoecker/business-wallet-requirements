# Company law research note (G1)

Read in full (OJ text via publications.europa.eu): Directive (EU) 2025/25 (all amending provisions), Directive (EU) 2019/1151 (Arts 13a-13j, 16, 16a, 19, 22, 24, 28a-28c, 30a), Directive 2017/1132 original Arts 14-24, 29 and 2019/2121 Arts 86m-86n, Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/1042 (Annex points 1-16), proposal COM(2026) 321 (EU Inc, 18.3.2026, Articles 15-31 read, rest skimmed). Consolidated 2017/1132 not obtainable (guessed version dates returned 404).

Duty-holders: directives bind Member States; registers, notaries, authorities and companies are bound through national transposition (2025/25: transposition 31 July 2027, application 31 July 2028; Art 19(2)(i) and 19b 2028/2029).

(a) Identification of representatives: 13b (eID means incl. EUDI Wallet since 2025/25; list public; physical presence case by case), 13g(3)(a),(b),(c) and 28a(3) (capacity, authority, identity, trust services), 16c(1) (verification by court/notary before a digital EU power of attorney is drawn up), 19(2)(g) and 18(4) (representatives, with EUID of legal-person representatives, free via BRIS).
(b) EUID: 16(1) (assignment, minimum elements), 2021/1042 Annex 9 (ISO 6523 structure; country code, register identifier, number, optional verification digit; point 8 data for platform), 29(4) branch identifier, 24(2)(f) mutual accessibility with BO and insolvency interconnections. Note 2025/25 now cites 2021/1042 point 9.
(c) EU Company Certificate (16b) and digital EU power of attorney (16c): trust-service authentication, wallet compatibility, acceptance in all Member States, no legalisation (16d), authenticity safeguards (16e, 16f), implementing acts under 24(2)(d),(e) due 31 July 2026 (not found published as of 3 Oct 2026; a late-September source says overdue).
(d) Electronic documents: 13j (online filing, origin and integrity verifiable), 15, 16(2),(6) (electronic, machine-readable storage), 16a(4),(5), 18(2) (standard message format).
(e) Cross-border: 13g(2a) and 28a(5a) once-only via BRIS, 18, 19, 22, 86n, 2021/1042 security (point 3), availability 98% (point 14).
