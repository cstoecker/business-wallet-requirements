Method: full text read from the Publications Office (publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>, xhtml/pdf; CELLAR SPARQL for consolidated versions, amending acts, corrigenda), Council register (data.consilium.europa.eu), Parliament Legislative Observatory and Commission digital-strategy page (these three via WebFetch summary, so "read (summary)"). Read-state key: READ = full text read by me; READ-PART = relevant articles read; SUMMARY = page fetched through WebFetch summarizer; CELLAR = metadata query only; NOT READ = not verified.

## 0. Key finding: omnibus numbering
The brief's "COM(2025) 836 / 837" are two different proposals:
- COM(2025) 836 (2025/0359(COD)) = Digital Omnibus on AI. ADOPTED as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of 8 July 2026 (OJ L 24.7.2026, in force 27.7.2026). READ.
- COM(2025) 837 (2025/0360(COD)) = Digital Omnibus (GDPR, ePrivacy, Data Act, NIS2/CER/DORA/eIDAS incident reporting single entry point). NOT adopted. READ (proposal text).
The repo's SRC-SWD-2025-836 and SRC-EC-SWD-2025-837 are SWD numbers; do not confuse with the COM numbers.
