# RES-evidence-claims-1: primary-source support for the claims in docs/16 ("Claims that need support before the talk")

Scope: facts from primary sources only (specification text, standards-body pages, official regulation text); no recommendation. Date of work: 2026-10-04. Covers five of the eight claims: UNTP, EPCIS/CBV/Digital Link, W3C VC 2.0 and adoption outside Europe, EU DPP legal basis, CEN/CENELEC JTC 24. Not covered: Catena-X credential share, ECLASS/IDTA licences, JSON-LD performance, GDPR Art 22/AI Act limits, scope.
Source IDs reuse `_data/graph/sources.yml` where they exist (SRC-VCDM-2.0, SRC-ESPR, SRC-EC-DPP-PAGE, SRC-GS1-VC-DM). SRC-CIR-2026-1778 is in /tmp/review/RES-dec12-new_sources.yml (not yet in the register). Other sources are (new) in /tmp/review/RES-evidence-claims-1-new_sources.yml.
Read states: full = whole page/section read; part = section read, rest not; snippet = web search result text only; not read.
Method caveat: web pages were read through a summarising fetch tool (WebFetch), so quotes marked "(fetch)" are the tool's rendering of the page and must be re-checked against the page before use on a slide. EUR-Lex texts (ESPR, CIR 2026/1778) were downloaded as XHTML from publications.europa.eu and read directly (quotes exact). GS1 PDFs were converted to text and checked directly.

## Gaps and flags

- UNTP version inconsistency (0.7.0 vs 0.8.0) and absence of a stated UN/CEFACT Forum approval (1.4, 1.5). Claim "UN/CEFACT standard" is supported by the self-description only.
- UNTP requires VC-JOSE-COSE and did:web; ESPR and CIR 2026/1778 name none of these (4.9, 4.14). The link from DPP to VC rests on ESPR Art 11 "digital credentials" (4.6), without a format.
- CIR 2026/1778 Art 12(2)(a) gives the legal hook for typed links DPP to evidence (4.12).
- Outside Europe: only DHS SVIP and the W3C implementation report are primary and citable; Canada, Japan, Korea, China not found at specification level (3.8 to 3.11).
- CEN: standards themselves not read; OJ citation list and the status of EN 18239/18246 after 16 July 2026 not found.
- Web pages were read via a summarising fetch tool; quotes marked (fetch) need a direct re-read.
