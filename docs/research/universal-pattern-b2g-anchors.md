## 0. Headline findings the slide must respect

1. **The customs anchor has moved.** COM(2023) 258 is adopted: Regulation (EU) 2026/2108 of 16 September 2026 "establishing the Union Customs Code and the European Union Customs Authority, and repealing Regulation (EU) No 952/2013" (OJ L, 19.9.2026). Reg 952/2013 stays the law applied until the new Regulation applies on 21 September 2027 (Art 287(2)); its repeal in Art 284(1) is not in the list of early-applying provisions in Art 287(3) (my reading, calc). A slide that cites 952/2013 as "the" anchor is out of date. Cite both, with dates.
2. **The EU Customs Data Hub and EUCA are legislated, not proposed.** Hub: Arts 35-39 apply from entry into force (20.9.2026, calc); mandatory use for e-commerce importers from 1 July 2028, voluntary for others from 1 March 2031, mandatory for all from 1 March 2034; all functionalities fully operational by 1 February 2034 (Art 285). EUCA: seat Lille (Art 230), established with Arts 229-231 applying from entry into force.
3. **Customs and market surveillance read the DPP registry, they do not write the passport.** ESPR Art 13(6), Art 15 and CIR 2026/1778 give both authorities read access. No operative article read gives an authority a right to write into a passport or registry record. Only recital 1 of CIR 2026/1778 says authorities "should be able to update or change the status". ESPR Art 9(2)(g) delegates the write-rights question to each delegated act. Treat "authorities write" as unproven on the text.
4. **Signatures are mostly silent in the B2G texts.** The only hard seal/signature duties read are: CIR 2026/1778 Art 4 (identity of registrant by qualified seal or qualified EAA) and Art 9(3) (proof of registration sealed), and the original text of Dir 2017/1132 Art 16(4) (certified copies, advanced electronic signature). Customs (952/2013, 2026/2108), 2019/1020, DGA, Data Act, OOTS and CSW-CERTEX require integrity and non-repudiation as system properties without prescribing a signature or seal on the evidence object.
5. **No read text requires linked data or RDF.** The nearest: CIR 2026/1778 Art 12(2)(a) "typed and resolvable links", Art 12(3) DCAT-AP metadata. DCAT-AP itself is an RDF-based W3C DCAT profile (v3.0.1).

## 6. Where the texts are plainly silent (state this on the slide)

- UCC 952/2013 and Reg 2026/2108 (articles read): no electronic signature or seal duty on declarations or supporting documents; no linked data.
- Reg 2022/2399: no signature or seal on supporting documents; no linked data.
- Reg 2019/1020: no signature, no identifier scheme beyond name and address; no linked data.
- ESPR Arts 9-15: states authentication, reliability and integrity as outcomes, does not say how; no format beyond "open standards ... interoperable format ... machine-readable".
- CIR 2026/1778: no data carrier rule; no passport content in the registry (only identifiers, commodity codes, hash); no serialisation; no authority write right in the articles.
- DGA, Data Act: no signature, no identifier, no RDF.
- OOTS: integrity and non-repudiation yes; signature no; formats XML-based.
- BO registers: nothing read on entry-level integrity.
