# Manufacturing-X

## What it is and what it does

Manufacturing-X is a cross-industry industrial-policy initiative to build a decentrally organised, interoperable data space, the "Datenraum Industrie 4.0", aiming at digital sovereignty, resilience, sustainability and competitiveness. The **Plattform Industrie 4.0 Task Force Manufacturing-X** published the whitepaper (BMWK, November 2022). Its stated goal is that companies connect once to collaborate across ecosystems rather than connecting separately to each data ecosystem. <span class="vtag v-ok">verified</span> ([whitepaper](https://www.plattform-i40.de/IP/Redaktion/DE/Downloads/Publikation/Manufacturing-X_lang.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=6), [MX-Talk 24_10](https://www.plattform-i40.de/IP/Redaktion/DE/Downloads/Publikation/MX-Talk15.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=1))

It is a **portfolio** of funded ecosystems rather than one product: Catena-X, Factory-X, Aerospace-X, Robot-X, Decide4Eco, HealthTrack-X, Semiconductor-X, SCALE-MX and Chemie-X <span class="vtag v-ok">verified</span>. An MX Guidance Board aligns the ecosystems for interoperability and common standards <span class="vtag v-ok">verified</span> (Factory-X site).

## Identity and trust: a gap

The December 2024 analysis found that few projects were sure how they would implement authentication; different solutions were in use (BPN, GXDCH, webID, eIDAS, IATP, SSI) and their compatibility, as well as discovery services, still had to be examined <span class="vtag v-ok">verified</span>. **No binding Manufacturing-X-wide identity standard** was found in the sources read. This is exactly the gap a European Business Wallet could close.
