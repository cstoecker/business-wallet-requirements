# CIRPASS-2

## What it is and what it does

CIRPASS-2 is an Innovation Action funded by the Digital Europe Programme, running from May 2024 to April 2027 and coordinated by CEA-List (France). It aims to "demonstrate functioning DPPs in real settings and at scale" through **13 lighthouse pilots in 4 value chains**: textiles, electrical and electronic equipment, tyres and construction. Batteries are not among them. ✅ ([about](https://cirpass2.eu/about/), [pilots](https://cirpass2.eu/lighthouse-pilots/))

Its site lists cross-pilot interoperability, DPP vocabularies and ontologies, and expert working groups. The predecessor project CIRPASS defines a DPP as a structured collection of product data with agreed data management and access rights, conveyed through a unique identifier and accessible via a data carrier; access is role-dependent (🔎 seen only as search snippets of the CIRPASS D2.2 and D3.2 deliverables).

## Relation to the European Business Wallet

No CIRPASS-2 source read mentions wallets or verifiable credentials, and no link to the EBW was found. 🔎 Why it matters here: DPP access rights per stakeholder type, authentication and transfer of responsibility are exactly the problems legal-person identity and mandates address.
