# energy data-X

> **Disclosure:** Spherity, the originator of this site, is a core partner of energy data-X.

## What it is and what it does

energy data-X is a data ecosystem (data space) for the energy industry based on Gaia-X standards. It started on 1 October 2023, is led by **TenneT** and funded by BMWK under the 7th Energy Research Programme (EUR 9.5 m). The planned project end is September 2026 with scaling planned from Q3 2026; the current status is not verified. <span class="vtag v-ok">verified</span> <span class="vtag v-todo">to verify</span> ([project site](https://www.energydata-x.eu/en/), [TenneT MX-Talk 25_7](https://www.plattform-i40.de/IP/Redaktion/DE/Downloads/Publikation/MX-Talk21-energydataX.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3))

Following the DSSC blueprint, it provides **five federated services** (<span class="vtag v-ok">verified</span> MX-Talk): connector (data sovereignty), compliance, service offering and federated catalogue, portal/API and clearing house (registration, onboarding, machine-to-machine access), and **identity and trust**: who a participant is and which market role it holds. Market roles follow the BDEW role model.

On 11 June 2026 dena's Future Energy Lab, energy data-X and Fraunhofer IEE demonstrated the first data exchange between two energy data spaces (aggregated, anonymised smart-metering data for balance-group management). <span class="vtag v-ok">verified</span>
