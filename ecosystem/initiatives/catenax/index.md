# Catena-X

## What it is and what it does

Catena-X describes itself as "the first globally trusted and collaborative data ecosystem for the automotive industry", for exchanging data with direct business partners along the supply chain. The **Catena-X Automotive Network Association** is its governance body; members define business areas and develop the standards, published in releases (CX-Titan stable, CX-Neptune preview). ✅ [catena-x.net](https://catena-x.net/en/)

Technology-neutral view of the services it standardises (all ✅ from the cited standards, preview versions):
- **Data-space connectivity:** participants exchange data via the Dataspace Protocol, with catalogue, contract negotiation and transfer endpoints (CX-0018).
- **Identity and trust:** participants are identified by a DID; credentials are exchanged under the Decentralized Claims Protocol; access depends on membership, business-partner and framework-agreement credentials (CX-0018, CX-0049).
- **Onboarding:** an onboarding service provider collects company data, a core service provider validates it and issues the credentials (CX-0006).
- **Semantic models:** data-model and use-case standards per business area.

Eclipse Tractus-X is the open-source implementation community; it is context only, not a requirement source.

## Relation to the European Business Wallet

Today's mandatory model is a single trust anchor (the core service provider). The association states it is "evolving from a single trust anchor to a federated trust model and leading the integration of overarching trust infrastructures such as the European Business Wallet" ✅. A **self-sovereign identity / digital identity expert group** (start August 2026) will advise whether Catena-X should act as consumer, contributor or co-designer in the eIDAS 2.0 / EBW ecosystem ✅ ([call](https://catena-x.net/news/apply-now-expert-group-self-sovereign-identity-ssi-digital-identity/)). Integration is therefore a plan, not yet a published requirement.

**Version caveat.** The standards read are the CX-Neptune *preview* ("not eligible for certification yet"); stable CX-Titan versions of CX-0050, CX-0149, CX-0010 and CX-0001 still have to be fetched, and the CX-0050 and CX-0149 pages returned 404 when retrieved. 🔎
