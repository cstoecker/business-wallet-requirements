  1. Identification and e-signature in consumer flows are accessible
draft

EBW-NFR-062: Identification and e-signature in consumer flows are accessible

Category
NFR, Non-functional
Provenance
D (derived); legal status: in force
Plane
trust
Perspectives
B2C
Actors
Service provider, Wallet provider
Verification
test
Review
Reviewer: pending. Last verified: 2026-10-03. Analysis, not legal advice.

Requirement

Where identification methods or electronic signatures are provided as part of an e-commerce or consumer banking service, they shall be perceivable, operable, understandable and robust.

Rationale

Derived mapping: when a wallet authentication or signing function is used inside a consumer e-commerce or consumer banking transaction (an inference, as the wallet is a business instrument), the Directive's wording applies to that function.

Sources

  • Directive (EU) 2019/882 on the accessibility requirements for products and services (European Accessibility Act) (OJ original text of 17 April 2019 (consolidation not read; no later amendment was checked)2019-04-17), Annex I, Section IV, points (e)(i) and (g)(iii). SRC-DIR-2019-882

providing identification methods, electronic signatures, and payment services which are perceivable, operable, understandable and robust
