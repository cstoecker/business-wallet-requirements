Requirements

Each requirement is atomic, technology-neutral and traced to a source. They are grouped into 14 categories and can be filtered by category, status, provenance and text. How they are written and reviewed is described in the requirements management methodology.

Draft. 819 requirements are derived from sources that were read in the original text. None has been reviewed by a named person yet, and those marked "proposal" follow the Commission proposal COM(2025) 838, which may change. Another 27 statements from ecosystems are candidates that still have to be derived.
