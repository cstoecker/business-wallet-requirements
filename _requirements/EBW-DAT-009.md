req_id: EBW-DAT-009
title: Verify third-party status with no more information than necessary
category: DAT
statement: For the purpose of verifying whether a natural or legal person qualifies as a user or as a third party, the user or third party shall not be required to provide any information beyond what is necessary, and the data holder shall not keep information on the third party’s access beyond what is necessary.
rationale: Limits both the identity evidence requested from a data recipient and the retention of access records.
