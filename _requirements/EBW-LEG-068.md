req_id: EBW-LEG-068
title: Owner ensures measures for processing by its users
category: LEG
statement: An owner that is the controller shall ensure appropriate technical and organisational measures, including training and information of the users it authorises, so that processing by those users in the wallet complies with the data protection rules.
rationale: Processing by employees within an organisation is presumed to take place under the organisation control, so the duty to ensure compliance sits with the owner. Source is an EDPB guideline (not binding); the underlying duty is Article 24(1) GDPR.
