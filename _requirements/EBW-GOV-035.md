req_id: EBW-GOV-035
title: Companies to file changes within 15 working days
category: GOV
statement: Member States shall ensure that any changes to the documents and information regarding companies are filed with the register within a period not exceeding 15 working days from the date the changes were made.
rationale: Sets a time limit that a wallet-based filing workflow and reminders for companies have to respect. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
