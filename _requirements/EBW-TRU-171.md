req_id: EBW-TRU-171
title: Identity proofing covers the representative of a third-country operator
category: TRU
statement: A provider of owner identification data shall issue owner identification data and a unique identifier to an economic operator established outside the Union only if the identity proofing of the operator and of its representative empowered to carry out the onboarding fulfils the identity verification methods of Article 24(1a) of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014.
rationale: The Council text extends the proofing condition of the Commission proposal (EBW-TRU-015) to the empowered representative, which links the legal-person identity to a natural person in a third country.
