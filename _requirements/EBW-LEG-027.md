title: Trust service providers notify significant incidents within 24 hours
category: LEG
statement: When a significant incident has an impact on the provision of its trust services, the trust service provider shall notify the CSIRT or competent authority without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.
rationale: The trust service deadline is shorter than the 72 hours that applies to other entities.
