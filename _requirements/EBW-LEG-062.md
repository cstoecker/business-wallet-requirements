req_id: EBW-LEG-062
title: Document any fundamental-alteration or burden assessment
category: LEG
statement: Where an economic operator relies on fundamental alteration or disproportionate burden, it shall document the assessment, keep the results for five years after the service was last provided, and renew the burden assessment at least every five years.
rationale: The only route to a lawful accessibility exception is a documented and refreshed assessment; a wallet provider that relies on it must keep the evidence.
