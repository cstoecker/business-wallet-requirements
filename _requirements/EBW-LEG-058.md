title: Accessibility information for services in terms and conditions
category: LEG
statement: The service provider shall include in its general terms and conditions, or an equivalent document, information assessing how the service meets the accessibility requirements, and shall make that information available to the public in written and oral format, including in a manner accessible to persons with disabilities.
rationale: Tells users and authorities how the service meets accessibility rules; for a wallet it is a documentation and publication duty on the provider.
