req_id: EBW-AIF-066
title: Overseers can remain aware of automation bias
category: AIF
statement: The provider of a high-risk AI system shall provide it to the deployer in such a way that the natural persons assigned to human oversight are enabled, as appropriate and proportionate, to remain aware of the possible tendency of automatically relying or over-relying on the output of the system.
rationale: Approval prompts for agent actions invite reflexive consent; this is the Union-law basis for designing them so that approvers stay alert. Applies only if the system is high-risk under Article 6; mapping to an agent is a derivation.
