title: Registration certificates carry a general access policy and privacy URL
category: TRU
statement: Where a Member State authorises registration certificates, the issued certificates shall express each intended use, include a harmonised general access policy limiting requests to the registered data, and contain a URL to the privacy policy for the intended use.
rationale: Lets wallets detect over-asking and show the privacy policy.
