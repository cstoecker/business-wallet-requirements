req_id: EBW-LEG-079
title: Users can remove all data and settings from a product permanently
category: LEG
statement: The manufacturer of a device or software that is a product with digital elements shall, where applicable, ensure that users can securely and easily remove permanently all data and settings and, where the data can be transferred to other products or systems, that the transfer is secure.
rationale: Supports decommissioning of a device that holds wallet keys or credentials; role is the manufacturer under Article 13(1), and the duty is subject to the risk assessment ("where applicable"). Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 applies from 11 December 2027.
