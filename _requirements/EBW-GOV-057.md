title: Limit generic and shared accounts so that users stay identifiable
category: GOV
statement: Where the wallet owner is a financial entity, its access control policy shall include a provision on user accountability that limits, to the extent possible, the use of generic and shared user accounts and ensures that users are identifiable for the actions performed.
rationale: Derived; a shared technical account for several machines or employees would break attribution of acts to the owner in the log.
