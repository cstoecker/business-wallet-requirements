req_id: EBW-NFR-068
title: Wallet units meet Web Accessibility Directive requirements
category: NFR
statement: Wallet Providers shall ensure that their Wallet Units comply with the applicable requirements and standards in the directive on the accessibility of websites and mobile applications of public sector bodies.
rationale: The EUDI ARF adds the Web Accessibility Directive for wallet units beyond the Directive 2019/882 duty in eIDAS Article 5a(21); the text cites "Directive 2016/2012" and the note cites EN 301 549 V1.1.2, both apparently outdated or mistyped (the Directive is 2016/2102, the published version V3.2.1), so a business wallet should follow the Directive and the OJ-referenced standard.
