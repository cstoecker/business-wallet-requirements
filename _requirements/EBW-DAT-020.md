req_id: EBW-DAT-020
title: Tools to give and withdraw consent and permissions
category: DAT
statement: A data intermediation services provider that provides tools for obtaining consent from data subjects or permissions from data holders shall provide data subjects with tools to give and withdraw consent and data holders with tools to give and withdraw permissions to process data.
rationale: Withdrawal of permission is a first-class function, as is revocation of authorisations in business wallets.
