req_id: EBW-AIF-035
title: Deployer retains logs of high-risk AI system for six months
category: AIF
statement: The deployer of a high-risk AI system shall keep the logs automatically generated by that system, to the extent under its control, for a period appropriate to the intended purpose of at least six months.
rationale: An organisation running an AI agent against its business wallet must retain the agent's logs itself. Applies only if the AI system is classified high-risk under Article 6; mapping to a wallet or agent operator is a derivation.
