title: Joint representation requires two or more signatories acting together
category: TRU
statement: Where the representation type of the authorised signatories of a legal entity is joint, the relying party shall treat a binding commitment as made on behalf of the entity only when two or more signatories have acted together.
rationale: Derived from the code-list meaning of JOINT in the authorised signatories rulebook, which gives the definition but no verifier duty; it is the nearest source for multi-user authorisation of a single act.
