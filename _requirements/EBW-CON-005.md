req_id: EBW-CON-005
title: Wallet authorisations do not create or limit a legal mandate
category: CON
statement: Authorisations granted through the authorisation system of a European Business Wallet shall be treated as technical and shall not create, limit or otherwise affect any power of attorney or legal mandate under national or Union law.
rationale: Derived from recital 18 of the Council text (a recital, not an operative duty); it prevents the wallet from being used as proof of legal representation beyond national law.
