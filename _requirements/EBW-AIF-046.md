req_id: EBW-AIF-046
title: Fundamental rights impact assessment before deployment
category: AIF
statement: Before deploying a high-risk AI system referred to in Article 6(2), a deployer that is a body governed by public law or a private entity providing public services shall perform an assessment of the impact on fundamental rights, and shall notify its results to the market surveillance authority.
rationale: Public bodies and public-service entities adopting AI around wallet-mediated B2G processes would trigger this assessment. Applies only if the AI system is classified high-risk under Article 6; mapping to a wallet or agent operator is a derivation. Chapter III Sections 1-3 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 apply from 2 December 2027 (Annex III systems) or 2 August 2028 (Annex I systems) under Article 113 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.
