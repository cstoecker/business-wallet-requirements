req_id: EBW-AIF-065
title: Overseers can understand capacities and limits and monitor operation
category: AIF
statement: The provider of a high-risk AI system shall provide it to the deployer in such a way that the natural persons assigned to human oversight are enabled, as appropriate and proportionate, to understand its relevant capacities and limitations and to monitor its operation, including to detect and address anomalies, dysfunctions and unexpected performance.
rationale: A person who approves or revokes an agent's authority needs this information to do so. Applies only if the system is high-risk under Article 6; mapping to an agent is a derivation (dates as in the Article 14(3) requirement).
