req_id: EBW-LEG-077
title: Delegates of EU Inc. directors are bound by directors' legal obligations
category: LEG
statement: An individual to whom the directors of an EU Inc. company have delegated powers shall adhere to the legal obligations imposed on directors when exercising the delegated powers.
rationale: Delegation by directors does not remove the legal duties attached to the power, which a wallet mandate for a delegate has to respect.
