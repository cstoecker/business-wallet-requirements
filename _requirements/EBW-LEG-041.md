req_id: EBW-LEG-041
title: Human ability to interrupt a high-risk AI system
category: LEG
statement: A high-risk AI system acting with wallet credentials shall allow assigned natural persons to intervene in its operation or interrupt it through a stop mechanism that brings it to a safe state.
rationale: Revocation or suspension of an agent's mandate is the wallet-side means of enforcing this stop. Chapter III Sections 1-3 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 apply from 2 December 2027 (Annex III systems) or 2 August 2028 (Annex I systems) under Article 113 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.
