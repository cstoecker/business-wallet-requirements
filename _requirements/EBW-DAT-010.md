title: Data shared on request of the user or a party acting for the user
category: DAT
statement: Upon request by a user, or by a party acting on behalf of a user, the data holder shall make readily available data and the relevant metadata available to a third party without undue delay, in a comprehensive, structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
rationale: The law accepts a party acting on behalf of the user, so a data-space connector must be able to verify that representation (mandate).
