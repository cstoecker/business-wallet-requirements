title: Documents provided by the wallet meet EN 301 549 clause 10
category: NFR
statement: Where a wallet provides downloadable documents that are not web pages, such as receipts, notices or reports, they shall conform to the applicable requirements of clause 10 of EN 301 549 V3.2.1.
rationale: Delivery receipts, evidence and reports are documents; the standard treats downloadable documents separately from web pages, and V3.2.1 added them to the public sector mapping.
