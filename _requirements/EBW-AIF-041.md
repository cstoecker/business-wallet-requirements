title: Post-market monitoring system for high-risk AI
category: AIF
statement: The provider of a high-risk AI system shall establish and document a post-market monitoring system, proportionate to the technology and risks, that actively and systematically collects and analyses performance data throughout the system's lifetime.
rationale: Continuous monitoring of deployed agent behaviour is the feedback loop to the risk management system. Applies only if the AI system is classified high-risk under Article 6; mapping to a wallet or agent operator is a derivation.
