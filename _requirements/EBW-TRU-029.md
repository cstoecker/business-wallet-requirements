title: Wallet unit attestation signed under a listed certificate
category: TRU
statement: The wallet provider shall issue wallet unit attestations for each wallet unit and shall sign or seal them so that the signatures or seals can be validated by means of a certificate listed in accordance with Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2980.
rationale: Lets issuers and relying parties authenticate the wallet unit against a trust anchor; the duty now sits in Article 6(1), in force from 11 August 2026 (consolidated text 02024R2979-20260811).
