req_id: EBW-LEG-080
title: Device records access to and modification of data, services and functions
category: LEG
statement: The manufacturer of a product with digital elements shall, where applicable, ensure that the product provides security-related information by recording and monitoring relevant internal activity, including access to or modification of data, services or functions, with an opt-out mechanism for the user.
rationale: Gives a device-level source for a machine's own activity record, a precondition for attributing the machine's acts to its owner; role is the manufacturer under Article 13(1).
