req_id: EBW-AIF-064
title: Oversight measures commensurate with the level of autonomy
category: AIF
statement: The provider of a high-risk AI system shall identify human oversight measures that are commensurate with the risks, level of autonomy and context of use of the system, and shall either build them into the system where technically feasible or specify them for the deployer to implement.
rationale: Ties the strength of oversight to how autonomous an agent is, which is the basis for mandate-defined limits. Applies only if the system is high-risk under Article 6; mapping to an agent is a derivation. Chapter III Sections 1-3 apply from 2 December 2027 or 2 August 2028 under Article 113 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.
