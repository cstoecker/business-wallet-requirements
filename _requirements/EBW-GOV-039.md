title: Update the self-assessment every 24 months or on trigger
category: GOV
statement: The provider of European Business Wallets shall update its self-assessment every 24 months, and immediately where a significant incident occurred, a substantial change was made to architecture, security functions, cryptographic components, critical suppliers or core functionalities, a function affecting security, validity, authenticity or portability was suspended, revoked or materially modified, or the risk assessment identifies a new risk.
rationale: It keeps the conformity evidence current when keys, devices or suppliers change.
