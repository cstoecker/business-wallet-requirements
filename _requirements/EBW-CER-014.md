title: Vulnerability assessment of the secure device at AVA_VAN.5
category: CER
statement: National certification schemes shall require the evaluation of a wallet secure cryptographic device that is within the object of certification to include a vulnerability assessment at level AVA_VAN.5 of EN ISO/IEC 15408-3, unless a lower level is duly justified to the certification body.
rationale: It names the depth of evaluation expected for the hardware that holds the keys.
