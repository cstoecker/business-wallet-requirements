title: Deployer uses high-risk AI per instructions for use
category: AIF
statement: The deployer of a high-risk AI system shall take appropriate technical and organisational measures to use the system in accordance with its instructions for use.
rationale: An enterprise using an AI agent with its business wallet must stay within the provider's intended purpose. Applies only if the AI system is classified high-risk under Article 6; mapping to a wallet or agent operator is a derivation.
