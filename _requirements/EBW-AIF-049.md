req_id: EBW-AIF-049
title: Safeguards for Article 22 exceptions and special-category data
category: AIF
statement: Where a controller relies on contract necessity or explicit consent for a decision based solely on automated processing with legal or similarly significant effects, the controller shall implement suitable safeguards including the rights to obtain human intervention, to express a point of view and to contest the decision, and shall not base the decision on special categories of data unless Article 22(4) conditions are met.
rationale: Adds the Art 22(2) exception structure and the Art 22(4) special-category limit to EBW-LEG-015, which covers only paragraphs (1) and (3); relevant to agents deciding on wallet-held personal data.
