req_id: EBW-AIF-029
title: AI literacy of staff operating AI systems
category: AIF
statement: The provider and the deployer of an AI system used in or with a business wallet shall take measures to support the development of AI literacy of their staff and other persons dealing with the operation and use of the AI system on their behalf.
rationale: Applies to every AI system regardless of risk class, so a wallet provider or agent operator using AI must train its operators. Mapping to wallet provider or agent operator is a derivation (D).
