req_id: EBW-LEG-072
title: Agreements recognising third-country trust services require equivalent requirements
category: LEG
statement: An agreement under which trust services of a third country are recognised as legally equivalent to qualified trust services shall ensure that the requirements applicable to qualified trust service providers in the Union and their qualified services are met by the third-country providers and services.
rationale: Article 14(2)(b) adds reciprocity (Union qualified services recognised in the third country); only point (a) is stated here. Without such an agreement a third-country seal or signature has no qualified effect.
