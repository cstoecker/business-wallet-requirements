title: Third-country wallet equivalence does not replace GDPR transfer rules
category: LEG
statement: Where the exchange of attestations of attributes with a third-country wallet or framework recognised under Article 17 involves a transfer of personal data, the controller shall ensure that the conditions of Chapter V of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 are met.
rationale: 'Derived: equivalence of assurances under Article 17 weighs data protection standards but is not an adequacy decision under GDPR Article 45, so it cannot replace Chapter V.'
