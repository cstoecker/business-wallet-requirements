title: Register copies and extracts compatible with the EUDI Wallet
category: INT
statement: Member States shall ensure that electronic copies and extracts of documents and information provided by the register are compatible with the European Digital Identity Wallet.
rationale: Register output must be storable and presentable from wallets, including business wallets built on the same format. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
