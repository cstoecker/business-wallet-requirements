title: No legalisation for register documents, EUCC and power of attorney
category: LEG
statement: Member States shall ensure that the EU Company Certificate, the digital EU power of attorney and certified copies and extracts provided by a register are exempted from all forms of legalisation and any similar formality.
rationale: Cross-border acceptance of these documents without extra formalities. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
