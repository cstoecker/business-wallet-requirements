title: Provider supplies owner name, identifier, address and country to the Commission
category: FUN
statement: Providers of European Business Wallets shall provide to the Commission, upon the issuance of a European Business Wallet to an owner, the official name of the owner as stated in the national register, the unique identifier, the unique digital address or addresses and the country of establishment.
rationale: The Council text fixes the minimum data for the European Digital Directory at issuance, so that the unique identifier is bound to the owner and to the digital address (EBW-FUN-002 covers only later updates).
