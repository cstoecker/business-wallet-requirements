req_id: EBW-FUN-047
title: No repeat requests for data already held in another register
category: FUN
statement: Member States shall ensure that a company forming a company or registering a branch in another Member State is not subject to requests for documents and information available in the register of the Member State where it is registered.
rationale: Once-only principle for company data; wallet-held company attestations complement but do not replace retrieval via the register interconnection. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
