title: Implementing act on unique identifiers within one year
category: GOV
statement: The Commission shall, by means of implementing acts adopted within one year after entry into force of the Regulation, establish specifications, requirements and procedures for the unique identifier created where no European Unique Identifier exists, including measures ensuring that no owner is attributed more than one unique identifier.
rationale: The Council text sets a one-year deadline for the identifier specification that the Commission proposal (EBW-INT-007) leaves open; the EBWOID format depends on it.
