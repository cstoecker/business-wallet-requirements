req_id: EBW-TRU-093
title: Companies have an EUID
category: TRU
statement: Member States shall ensure that companies listed in Annexes II and IIB have a European unique identifier, the EUID, allowing them to be unequivocally identified in communications between registers through the system of interconnection of registers.
rationale: The EUID is the legal-person identifier on which business wallet owner identification and register lookups rely. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
