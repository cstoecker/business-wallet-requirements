title: Wallet mobile and desktop apps meet EN 301 549 clause 11
category: NFR
statement: Where a wallet interface is software that is not a web page, it shall conform to the applicable requirements of clause 11 of EN 301 549 V3.2.1, including interoperability with assistive technology and platform user preferences.
rationale: Wallet apps are mostly non-web software; clause 11 is where their accessibility is specified, including following system contrast and font settings.
