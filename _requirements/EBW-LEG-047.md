req_id: EBW-LEG-047
title: Acceptance of digital EU power of attorney as evidence
category: LEG
statement: A body within the scope of Directive (EU) 2017/1132 shall accept the digital EU power of attorney as evidence of the authorised person's entitlement to represent the company as specified in the document.
rationale: Relying parties cannot demand parallel proofs of representation for procedures within the Directive's scope. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
