title: Arrangement between joint controllers
category: LEG
statement: Where an owner and a wallet provider jointly determine the purposes and means of a processing operation, they shall in a transparent manner determine their respective responsibilities by an arrangement and make its essence available to the data subject.
rationale: Joint determination of purposes and means makes both parties responsible towards users, so the split has to be written down.
