title: Account lifecycle distinguishes temporary deactivation from termination
category: OPS
statement: Where the wallet owner is a financial entity, its identity management shall include a lifecycle process for identities and accounts that covers creation, change, review, temporary deactivation and termination of all accounts.
rationale: Derived; the duty lies on financial entities. Temporary deactivation and termination are separate steps in the source, which supports modelling suspension of an authorisation apart from its revocation.
