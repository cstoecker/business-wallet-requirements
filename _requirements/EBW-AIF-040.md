req_id: EBW-AIF-040
title: Serious incident reporting for high-risk AI systems
category: AIF
statement: The provider of a high-risk AI system placed on the Union market shall report any serious incident to the market surveillance authorities of the Member State where it occurred, not later than 15 days after becoming aware, with shorter periods for widespread infringements and deaths.
rationale: AI-caused incidents involving wallet functions would be reportable in addition to cybersecurity incident duties. Applies only if the AI system is classified high-risk under Article 6; mapping to a wallet or agent operator is a derivation.
