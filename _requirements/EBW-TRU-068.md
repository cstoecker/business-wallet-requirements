title: Attestation issuer verifies the requester right to act
category: TRU
statement: Where applicable, the provider of a qualified or public sector attestation shall verify that the requester of the attestation has the right to act on behalf of the person that is the subject of the attestation.
rationale: Prevents attestations for a company being issued to a person without authority.
