title: Trusted list availability of 99,9 percent
category: OPS
statement: The scheme operator of a trusted list shall make the list available 24 hours a day and 7 days a week with an availability of at least 99,9 % over one year.
rationale: A relying party can only validate a trust service output if the list is reachable, so the standard fixes a minimum availability.
