title: Segregation of duties in access rights
category: GOV
statement: Where the wallet owner is a financial entity, it shall include in its access control policy the segregation of duties designed to prevent unjustified access to critical data and the allocation of combinations of access rights that may be used to circumvent controls.
rationale: Derived; the duty lies on financial entities and reaches the wallet through the owner's authorisation model. It is the source-backed basis for approval by a second person, which no Union text prescribes for wallet use as such.
