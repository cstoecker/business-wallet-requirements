title: BPN registered as an ISO/IEC 6523 identification scheme
category: INT
statement: The structure of the Business Partner Number shall be registered as an organisation identification scheme under ISO/IEC 6523 with the Registration Authority under an International Code Designator.
rationale: Makes the BPN interoperable with other registered organisation identifier schemes (for example LEI) and restricts issuance to one issuing organisation.
