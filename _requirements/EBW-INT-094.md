title: Branches of third-country companies disclosed with a unique identifier via BRIS
category: INT
statement: Member States shall ensure that the unique identifier of the branch of a company from a third country is available free of charge through the system of interconnection of registers.
rationale: Gives third-country companies with an EU branch an EU-side identifier; the identifier is that of the branch, not of the third-country company (Article 29(4) of Directive (EU) 2017/1132 as applied by Article 36(5), to verify).
