req_id: EBW-INT-069
title: Authority portals use EN 301 549 V3.2.1 as conformance basis
category: INT
statement: Public sector bodies shall use the harmonised standard EN 301 549 V3.2.1 (2021-03), whose reference is published in the Official Journal, as the basis for conformity of their websites and mobile applications with the Web Accessibility Directive.
rationale: Content meeting the published harmonised standard is presumed to conform (Directive Article 6(1)); Decision 2021/1339 replaced V2.1.2 with V3.2.1, so the target version is fixed by the Official Journal reference (use of the standard is voluntary, hence a derived design choice).
