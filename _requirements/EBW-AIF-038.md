req_id: EBW-AIF-038
title: Human can disregard, override or reverse AI output
category: AIF
statement: The provider of a high-risk AI system shall enable the natural persons assigned to human oversight to decide not to use the system or to disregard, override or reverse its output.
rationale: Complements EBW-LEG-041 (stop mechanism); an agent's proposed wallet action must be reversible or rejectable before effect. Applies only if the AI system is classified high-risk under Article 6; mapping to a wallet or agent operator is a derivation. Chapter III Sections 1-3 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 apply from 2 December 2027 (Annex III systems) or 2 August 2028 (Annex I systems) under Article 113 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.
