title: No wallet services to persons listed under EU asset-freeze measures
category: LEG
statement: The provider of European Business Wallets shall not make funds or economic resources available, directly or indirectly, to or for the benefit of natural or legal persons, entities or bodies listed in Annex I to Regulation (EU) No 269/2014, and shall assess whether wallet and owner identification services are economic resources within the meaning of that prohibition.
rationale: 'Derived: Article 2(2) of Regulation (EU) No 269/2014 prohibits making funds or economic resources available to listed persons and Article 17 applies it to EU-incorporated operators worldwide; that wallet services are economic resources is an interpretation to verify, and other EU sanctions regimes (for example Regulation (EU) No 833/2014) need the same check.'
