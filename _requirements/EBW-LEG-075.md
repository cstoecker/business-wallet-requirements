title: No wallet services to persons listed under EU asset-freeze measures
category: LEG
statement: The provider of European Business Wallets shall not make wallet or owner identification services available, directly or indirectly, to or for the benefit of a person or entity listed in Annex I to Regulation (EU) No 269/2014.
rationale: 'Derived: Article 2(2) bars making economic resources available to listed persons and applies to EU operators worldwide (Article 17); that wallet services are economic resources is an interpretation to verify, and other EU sanctions regimes (for example Regulation (EU) No 833/2014) need the same check.'
