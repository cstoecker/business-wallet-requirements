title: Authority websites and apps meet web accessibility principles
category: LEG
statement: Public sector bodies shall take the necessary measures to make their websites and mobile applications more accessible by making them perceivable, operable, understandable and robust.
rationale: Authority portals that receive wallet-based submissions or show wallet-derived data are public sector websites; the duty reaches Member State bodies, not the wallet provider (inference on the portal being in scope, see the research note).
