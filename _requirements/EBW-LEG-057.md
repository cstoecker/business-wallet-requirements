title: Consumer-facing services designed to EAA requirements
category: LEG
statement: Where a service falls under Article 2(2) of Directive (EU) 2019/882, the service provider shall design and provide the service in accordance with the accessibility requirements of that Directive.
rationale: The Directive binds economic operators for services provided to consumers; a business wallet provider is bound only if it also offers such a service (inference), for example a consumer-facing e-commerce or electronic communications service.
