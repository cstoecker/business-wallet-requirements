title: EU Company Certificate content on identity and representation
category: DAT
statement: The EU Company Certificate for limited liability companies shall include the name, legal form, registration number and Member State, EUID, registered office, correspondence address, status, and the persons authorised to represent the company and whether they act alone or jointly.
rationale: Defines the attribute set for company identification and representation in the certificate and hence in any wallet attestation based on it. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
