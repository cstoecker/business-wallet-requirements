title: Safeguards for solely automated decisions with legal effect
category: LEG
statement: Where a decision based solely on automated processing produces legal or similarly significant effects on a data subject and is necessary for a contract or based on explicit consent, the controller shall implement safeguards, at least human intervention, expressing a view and contesting it.
rationale: Art. 22(3) safeguards apply only to the Art. 22(2)(a) (contract) and (c) (explicit consent) exceptions; Art. 22(2)(b) (law) requires suitable measures laid down by that law, and Art. 22(1) is a data-subject right. Applying it to wallet functions or agents acting with wallet credentials and treating the operator as controller is a derivation. 'Natural person' = data subject.
