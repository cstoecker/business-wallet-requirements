req_id: EBW-LEG-015
title: Safeguards for solely automated decisions with legal effect
category: LEG
statement: Where a wallet function or an agent acting with wallet credentials makes a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects on a natural person, the controller shall provide at least human intervention, the chance to express a view and the right to contest the decision.
rationale: Delegated AI agents that accept or refuse transactions on a representative's behalf can trigger Article 22.
