req_id: EBW-NFR-066
title: No single biometric as the only authentication means
category: NFR
statement: Where the wallet uses biological characteristics, it shall not rely on a particular biological characteristic as the only means of user identification or for control of the wallet.
rationale: Users who lack a given characteristic would be excluded; the same idea appears in Annex I of Directive 2019/882 for products ("alternative to biometrics identification and control"), and it bears on wallet unlocking and user authentication.
