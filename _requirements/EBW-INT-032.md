req_id: EBW-INT-032
title: Listed formats are ETSI TS 119 472-1 clauses 2 to 6
category: INT
statement: The issuer of an attestation shall use a format conforming to clauses 2 to 6 of ETSI TS 119 472-1 V1.2.1 (2026-02), which cover the SD-JWT VC and ISO/IEC mdoc realisations.
rationale: The standards list that wallets must support names two realisations and leaves out JSON-LD W3C VC and X.509 attribute certificates.
