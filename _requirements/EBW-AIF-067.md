req_id: EBW-AIF-067
title: Employer informs workers before using a high-risk AI system at the workplace
category: AIF
statement: Before putting a high-risk AI system into service or using it at the workplace, a deployer who is an employer shall inform the workers' representatives and the affected workers that they will be subject to its use.
rationale: Applies where an employer deploys a high-risk agent that acts on or with employees' wallet authority; the information duty follows the rules and procedures of Union and national law on information of workers. Applies only if the system is high-risk under Article 6 (dates as in Article 14(3)).
