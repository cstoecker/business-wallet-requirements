req_id: EBW-TRU-172
title: Legal person not in a status that prevents it from acting
category: TRU
statement: For identity proofing of a legal person at assurance level high, the entity responsible for registration shall check that the legal person is not known by an authoritative source to be in a status that would prevent it from acting as that legal person.
rationale: The element is listed at level low and is cumulative for substantial and high; the existing requirement EBW-TRU-084 covers only name, legal form and unique identifier, not dissolved or insolvent entities.
