title: Assessment criteria before recognising a third-country wallet framework
category: GOV
statement: Before adopting an implementing act that recognises a third-country solution, system or framework as equivalent, the Commission shall assess it, taking into account at least data protection standards, compliance with cybersecurity requirements and the independence of the third-country system and its providers from the control of high-risk governments.
rationale: The Council text names the assessment criteria; the Commission proposal (EBW-INT-009) only requires an assessment of equivalence.
