req_id: EBW-TRU-132
title: Suspension of a qualified certificate is visible in status
category: TRU
statement: Where national rules provide for temporary suspension of a qualified certificate, the period of suspension shall be indicated in the certificate database and the suspension status shall be visible during the suspension from the service that gives status information.
rationale: Relying parties must be able to see a suspension while it lasts.
