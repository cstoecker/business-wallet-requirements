req_id: EBW-LEG-069
title: Wallet authorisation changes do not alter legal mandates
category: LEG
statement: Granting, changing or revoking an authorisation of a user in the European Business Wallet shall not by itself create, limit or revoke any power of attorney or legal mandate of that user.
rationale: Derived from Article 5(1)(j) (without prejudice to any power of attorney or legal mandate) and recital (17) (authorisations are of a technical nature); the Council text states no duty-holder, so the wallet provider is the derived addressee.
