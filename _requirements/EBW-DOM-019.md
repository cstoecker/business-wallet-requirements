title: eFTI operation logs identify the natural or legal person acting
category: DOM
statement: An eFTI platform shall record all data processing in operation logs that allow, as a minimum, identification of each distinct processing operation, the natural or legal person who made it and the sequence of operations on each data element.
rationale: Attributable logging, including legal-person identification, as an auditability duty at the platform.
