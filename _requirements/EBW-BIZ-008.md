title: Compensation for data access capped at cost for SME recipients
category: BIZ
statement: Where the data recipient is an SME or a not-for-profit research organisation without non-SME partner or linked enterprises, any compensation agreed for making data available shall not exceed the costs of making the data available.
rationale: Cost protection for small data recipients in data spaces; applies in business-to-business data sharing under Chapter III.
