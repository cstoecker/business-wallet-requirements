title: Evaluation of architectures with several secure devices
category: CER
statement: National certification schemes shall include evaluation activities that ensure the overall solution offers the expected level of security where an architecture uses several wallet secure cryptographic devices or performs some operations on critical assets outside the device.
rationale: Split-key and hosted-plus-local designs spread key operations over several components and need a combined evaluation.
