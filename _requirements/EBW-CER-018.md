req_id: EBW-CER-018
title: Enforce unproven assumptions on the operating environment
category: CER
statement: National certification schemes shall require, for each assumption on the operating environment of the wallet solution that is not backed by a certificate of conformity or other acceptable assurance information, a description of the mechanism the wallet provider uses to enforce the assumption and a justification that the mechanism is sufficient.
rationale: Where the device is supplied by the user, the provider must show how it enforces what it assumes about that device.
