title: 'Service websites and apps: perceivable, operable, robust'
category: NFR
statement: The service provider shall make the websites, including the related online applications, and the mobile device-based services, including mobile applications, through which a service is provided accessible in a consistent and adequate way by making them perceivable, operable, understandable and robust.
rationale: This is the core user interface duty of the Directive for services; for a wallet it covers the web console and the mobile app where those are part of a service in scope.
