req_id: EBW-TRU-069
title: Representation attestations short-lived or revocable
category: TRU
statement: The attestation provider issuing representation attestations shall ensure that either the attestations are short-lived or that all entities entitled by law to request revocation are able to do so.
rationale: Ends representative authority promptly when it lapses, for example when an employee leaves.
