req_id: EBW-LEG-071
title: Recognition of notified eID means across Member States
category: LEG
statement: Where electronic identification and authentication is required to access an online service of a public sector body, the Member State shall recognise an electronic identification means issued in another Member State under a listed scheme if its assurance level, substantial or high, is equal to or higher than the level the service requires.
rationale: Sets the cross-border assurance rule for representatives who authenticate to a business wallet or a public service with an eID means from another Member State; it does not cover private relying parties or third countries.
