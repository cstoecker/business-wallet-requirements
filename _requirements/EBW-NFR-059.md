title: Service information through more than one sensory channel
category: NFR
statement: The service provider shall make the information about the functioning of the service available via more than one sensory channel, present it in an understandable way and make its content available in text formats that can generate alternative assistive formats.
rationale: Users who cannot see or hear need the same service information in another form; it applies to wallet help and onboarding information.
