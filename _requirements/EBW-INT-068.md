title: Core functionality specifications refer to accessibility requirements
category: INT
statement: The standards and specifications defined in the implementing acts for the core functionalities of European Business Wallets shall refer to accessibility requirements.
rationale: The Commission's digital statement says accessibility is catered for through the implementing acts, as for the EUDI Wallet; the operative articles of COM(2025) 838 contain no accessibility clause, so the statement is the only anchor (hence derived).
