title: Delivery providers archive the evidence they issue
category: OPS
statement: The provider of an electronic registered delivery service shall archive the evidence or the evidence digests for each evidence it issued.
rationale: Evidence of sending and receipt is the product of the service, so it has to remain available to prove events later.
