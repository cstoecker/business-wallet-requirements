title: Evaluate revoking unit attestations within 24 hours of suspension
category: GOV
statement: Member States shall, no later than 24 hours after suspending a wallet solution, evaluate whether revoking the wallet unit attestations of the affected units, or any other additional remedy, is necessary.
rationale: It limits the time during which units of a suspended solution stay usable without a decision.
