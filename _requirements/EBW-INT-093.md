title: EUID given only for companies listed in Annex II
category: INT
statement: In a first level query on disqualification of a legal entity, the requesting Member State shall give the other registration number of the entity where it is not a company listed in Annex II to Directive (EU) 2017/1132; the EUID is given only for listed companies.
rationale: Shows that the EUID does not cover every legal entity, which is why the proposal needs a created unique identifier for other owners.
