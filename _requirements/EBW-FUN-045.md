title: European Digital Identity Wallet usable in online company procedures
category: FUN
statement: Member States shall ensure that a European Digital Identity Wallet can be used as an electronic identification means by applicants in the online procedures of Chapter III of Directive (EU) 2017/1132.
rationale: Gives the wallet of a natural-person representative a legal basis for formation, branch registration and filing; a business wallet must interoperate with it.
