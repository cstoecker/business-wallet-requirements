title: Processor contract for provider processing
category: LEG
statement: Where the wallet provider processes personal data on behalf of an owner acting as controller, the processing shall be governed by a binding contract or other legal act under which the provider processes the data only on documented instructions of the owner.
rationale: The owner is an organisation and its users are people, so the provider may be a processor for user-level data and then needs the Article 28 contract.
