title: Instructions for use in a digital format
category: AIF
statement: The provider of a high-risk AI system shall accompany the system with instructions for use in an appropriate digital format containing at least provider identity, performance characteristics and limitations, accuracy metrics, human oversight measures and log-handling mechanisms.
rationale: Deployers (including enterprises delegating wallet operations to an agent) need this information to use the system within its intended purpose. Applies only if the AI system is classified high-risk under Article 6; mapping to a wallet or agent operator is a derivation.
