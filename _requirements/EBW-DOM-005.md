title: Free and easy DPP access for actors per their access rights
category: DOM
statement: The digital product passport shall give customers, manufacturers, importers, distributors, dealers, professional repairers, independent operators, refurbishers, remanufacturers, recyclers, market surveillance authorities, customs authorities and other relevant actors free of charge and easy access, based on their respective access rights in the applicable delegated act.
rationale: Access is role-based and differs per actor class, so verifiers must be able to prove a role or an authority status to a DPP service.
