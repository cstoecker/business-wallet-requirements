req_id: EBW-GOV-034
title: Physical presence only case by case on suspected falsification
category: GOV
statement: Member States shall ensure that the physical presence of an applicant is required only on a case-by-case basis where there are reasons to suspect identity falsification, and that all other procedure steps can be completed online.
rationale: Limits fallback to in-person identification, so a remote identification via wallet remains the default path.
