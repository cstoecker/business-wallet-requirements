req_id: EBW-INT-092
title: Register notifications carry alternate identifiers such as the LEI
category: INT
statement: Register notifications exchanged through the system of interconnection of registers shall provide for zero or more alternate identifiers of the company, such as the Legal Entity Identifier, in addition to the mandatory EUID.
rationale: BRIS is the only official channel where the EUID and the LEI of a company are exchanged side by side; the LEI is optional there.
