title: Several directors of an EU Inc. represent the company jointly by default
category: LEG
statement: Where an EU Inc. company has several directors, the directors shall represent the company jointly.
rationale: Joint representation is the default rule; the individual-representation exception is in Article 43(2), covered by EBW-LEG-053. A wallet mandate model has to be able to express two or more signatories acting together (see EBW-TRU-168).
