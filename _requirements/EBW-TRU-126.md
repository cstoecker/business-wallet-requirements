req_id: EBW-TRU-126
title: Status shows only revoked or not revoked
category: TRU
statement: A status element used for person identification data, qualified electronic attestations of attributes or attestations issued by or on behalf of a public sector body responsible for an authentic source shall indicate only whether the attestation is revoked or not revoked and shall not support any other status value, such as suspension.
rationale: It rules out suspension for these credential types.
