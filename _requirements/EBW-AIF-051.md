title: Access right covers existence and logic of automated decisions
category: AIF
statement: Upon a data subject's access request, the controller shall confirm whether automated decision-making including profiling under Article 22(1) and (4) takes place and shall provide meaningful information about the logic involved and its consequences.
rationale: Wallet-side AI processing must be able to produce this information on request (the Article 14(2)(g) indirect-collection counterpart carries the same wording).
