req_id: EBW-LEG-074
title: Appropriate safeguards where no adequacy decision exists
category: LEG
statement: In the absence of an adequacy decision, a controller or processor shall transfer personal data to a third country or an international organisation only if it has provided appropriate safeguards and enforceable data subject rights and effective legal remedies are available.
rationale: Most third-country partners of a business wallet will lack an adequacy decision, so safeguards such as standard data protection clauses are the normal route.
