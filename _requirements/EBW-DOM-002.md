title: Request a missing unique operator identifier on behalf of the actor
category: DOM
statement: Where a unique operator identifier is not yet available for an actor, the economic operator that creates or updates the digital product passport shall request one on behalf of that actor and shall give that actor the full details once issued.
rationale: Shows that organisations without an identifier are onboarded by a third party acting for them, which a wallet onboarding flow has to support with an explicit mandate.
