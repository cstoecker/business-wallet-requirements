req_id: EBW-TRU-094
title: Registers issue EU Company Certificates accepted in all Member States
category: TRU
statement: Member States shall ensure that the registers issue EU Company Certificates, and that the certificate is accepted in all Member States as sufficient evidence, at the time of issuance, of the incorporation of the company and of the information it lists.
rationale: The certificate is the legally recognised company evidence a business wallet can store and present to relying parties. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
