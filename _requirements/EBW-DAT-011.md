req_id: EBW-DAT-011
title: Smart contracts for data sharing offer robustness and access control
category: DAT
statement: The vendor of an application using smart contracts to execute a data sharing agreement shall ensure that the smart contracts are designed with access control mechanisms and a very high degree of robustness to avoid functional errors and withstand manipulation by third parties.
rationale: Applies where a data-space connector executes agreements through smart contracts; conformity assessment and a declaration of conformity are required by Article 36(2).
