req_id: EBW-LEG-014
title: Export wallet data in a machine-readable format
category: LEG
statement: Where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means, the wallet provider shall give the data subject their provided personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and, where technically feasible, transmit them directly to another controller.
rationale: Portability supports changing wallet providers without losing the user's own data.
