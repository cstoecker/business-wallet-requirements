title: Economic operators register with the responsible customs authority
category: DOM
statement: Economic operators established in the customs territory of the Union shall register with the customs authorities responsible for the place where they are established.
rationale: Registration is the basis of the operator identity (EORI) that other Union acts such as the ESPR and the EUDR refer to; the UCC text read does not itself use the term EORI.
