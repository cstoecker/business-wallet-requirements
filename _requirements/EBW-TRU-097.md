req_id: EBW-TRU-097
title: Verification when granting a digital EU power of attorney
category: TRU
statement: Member States shall ensure that national requirements for drawing up, amending or revoking the digital EU power of attorney include at least the verification by courts, notaries or other competent authorities of the identity, legal capacity and authority to represent the company of the person granting, amending or revoking it.
rationale: Puts a trusted human or authority check ahead of every mandate used as wallet credential, relevant to mandate issuance. Member States apply the national measures from 31 July 2028 (Article 4(2) of Directive (EU) 2025/25).
