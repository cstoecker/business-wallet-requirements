req_id: EBW-LEG-059
title: Procedures keep the service accessible over time
category: LEG
statement: The service provider shall ensure that procedures are in place so that the provision of the service remains in conformity with the applicable accessibility requirements, taking changes in the service, in the requirements and in the harmonised standards into account.
rationale: Accessibility is lost through updates, so the provider needs a change process that re-checks conformity.
