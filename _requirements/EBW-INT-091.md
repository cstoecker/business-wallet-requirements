title: Unique identifier created on request or on wallet provision
category: INT
statement: Where an economic operator or public sector body has not been attributed a European Unique Identifier, a unique identifier for owner identification data shall be created upon its request or upon the provision of a European Business Wallet to it, in accordance with the Commission implementing act.
rationale: The Council text adds the two triggers for creation that the Commission proposal (EBW-INT-007) lacks; the text does not name who creates the identifier.
