req_id: EBW-CER-015
title: Device assumptions stay verified after certification
category: CER
statement: National certification schemes shall require evaluation activities that confirm, after issuance of the certificate, that the assumptions made on the wallet secure cryptographic device are still verified, and shall require applicants to describe the measures that keep them verified throughout the certification lifecycle.
rationale: Hardware not provided by the wallet provider can change after certification, and the assumption must be re-checked.
