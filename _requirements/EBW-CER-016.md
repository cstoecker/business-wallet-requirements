title: Unsuitable secure device blocks the certificate
category: CER
statement: National certification schemes shall deem a wallet secure cryptographic device unsuitable, so that the wallet solution is not issued a certificate of conformity, where verifications of the device remain inconclusive and compensating requirements for the wallet secure cryptographic application are not possible.
rationale: It states the consequence when the key custody hardware cannot be shown to meet the requirements.
