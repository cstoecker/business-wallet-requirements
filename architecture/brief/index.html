Stakeholder brief

This page condenses the catalogue into ten themes for a discussion with Industry 4.0, IPCEI-AI and IDTA stakeholders. Each theme has one question, the requirements that constrain the answer most, what the sources leave open, and one question for the session. It is a working paper for discussion. It takes no position on the options and is not legal advice.

How the selection works. A script picks priority P1 requirements that concern the business wallet, drops duplicates, takes at most three per theme and adds named conflict items (scripts/build_brief.py). The first selection is a proposal for review. Each item shows its source, its legal status (in force, proposal, standard, ecosystem) and its provenance (L law, S standard, D derived). Derived items are derivations, not law.

30 requirements in 10 themes. Decision codes link to the decision pages where they exist.

T1 Who is the wallet subject

Question
Do employees act for the company with their personal EUDI Wallet, with users and authorisations inside the organisation's business wallet, or with both?
Clusters
K02
Decision
DEC-02: Employees: personal EUDI Wallet, business wallet or both
Sources are silent on
How far the EUDI Wallet instruments apply to employees acting for a company; see the decision page for what was read.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-FUN-039The Member State shall ensure that the person identification data available from the electronic identification scheme uniquely represents the natural person, legal person or the natural person representing the natural or legal person, and is associated with the wallet.Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS), consolidated text as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, Article 5a(5)(f)in force, L
EBW-NFR-037The European Digital Identity Wallet shall leave the user in full control of the use of and of the data in the wallet.Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS), consolidated text as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, Article 5a(14), first sentencein force, L; needs-legal-review
EBW-FUN-007The European Business Wallet shall enable the owner to issue electronic attestations of attributes to European Business Wallets and European Digital Identity Wallets.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 5(1)(f)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Machine and plant operators act for a company across a supply chain; who signs, and for whom, decides liability.
  • IPCEI-AI: An agent needs a subject to act for; the same question reappears for agents (theme 9).
  • IDTA: Who is the asset owner or manufacturer in a signed passport or shell: the person, the company or the wallet.

Question for the session: Which actions must be done by a person in a personal wallet, and which may run under the organisation’s wallet?

T2 Delegation and mandates (employees, machines, agents)

Question
How do employees, machines (devices, plants, services) and AI agents get authority to act for an organisation: how is it created, limited, delegated onward, shared (four-eyes), suspended, revoked and attributed?
Clusters
K03, K16
Decision
DEC-13: Delegation and mandates for employees, machines and AI agents; see also DEC-02
Sources are silent on
The EBW texts name role-based authorisations (Council Art 5(1)(j)) but leave policy language and enforcement to implementing acts; the sources read say little about delegation chains, suspension as distinct from revocation, and authority of machines. See the decision page when it is published.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-FUN-009The European Business Wallet shall enable the use of qualified and non-qualified electronic attestations of attributes for owners and their authorised representatives to authenticate themselves.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 5(1)(h)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)
EBW-FUN-010The European Business Wallet shall enable the owner to authorise multiple users to access and operate the wallet and to manage and revoke such authorisations.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 5(1)(j)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)
EBW-FUN-011The European Business Wallet shall enable the owner to authorise relying parties to request electronic attestations of attributes issued to the owner and to manage and revoke such authorisations.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 5(1)(k)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Operators, plants and technical users act across company borders; authority must be limited, checkable and end when a person leaves or a machine is retired.
  • IPCEI-AI: An agent acts only within a mandate that a person or the organisation can see, limit and withdraw at once; actions are attributed to the owner.
  • IDTA: Who may sign, change or release a passport or shell entry for the manufacturer: person, system or agent, with which limits.

Question for the session: Which authority model is the common one for people, machines and agents, and where may it differ?

T3 Formats and meaning

Question
Which credential format carries which claim (SD-JWT VC, mdoc, JSON-LD), and how is meaning kept machine-readable for people, systems and agents?
Clusters
K01, K16
Decision
DEC-01: Credential data model, semantics and formats; see also DEC-10
Sources are silent on
The EBW proposal points to the format standards of CIR 2024/2979 Annex II (Art 8(3)); CIR 2026/1731 names SD-JWT VC and mdoc for the EUDI Wallet. The JSON-LD realisation (ETSI TS 119 472-1 clause 7) is outside the legal list.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-FUN-004The European Business Wallet shall enable the owner to selectively disclose European Business Wallet owner identification data and attributes contained in electronic attestations of attributes.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 5(1)(b)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)
EBW-INT-015The qualified electronic attestation of attributes shall contain a qualified indication, issuer data, subject data, attributes, validity period, unique attestation code, the issuer's qualified signature or seal, certificate location and status service location.Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS), consolidated text as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, Article 45d(1), Annex Vin force, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)
EBW-NFR-001The European Business Wallet shall enable the export of owner identification data, electronic attestations of attributes, communication logs and interaction records in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format at the owner's request or on termination of service or revocation of the provider's notification.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 5(1)(l)proposal, L

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Semantic identifiers and units must survive the step from credential to data space.
  • IPCEI-AI: Agents and retrieval need self-describing, linkable data; closed formats lose the context.
  • IDTA: Submodel semantics (ECLASS, IRDI) have to map to claims; the shell itself stays an implementation artefact.

Question for the session: Which semantic layer is the shared one, and which formats may carry it?

T4 Keys and control

Question
Where do an organisation's keys live (device, hosted back-end, owner HSM, remote seal) and what does that mean for control, assurance and exit?
Clusters
K04
Decision
DEC-03: Key custody and wallet architecture
Sources are silent on
The EBW texts say nothing about key location, key export between providers or an owner-run HSM.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-NFR-119Where a wallet provider holds private keys of an owner in its back-end, the wallet provider shall ensure that those keys are used only on the authorisation of the owner's authorised users and not by the wallet provider itself.Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS), consolidated text as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, Article 26(1)(c)in force, D; needs-legal-review
EBW-CER-009Where critical assets relate to performing electronic identification at assurance level substantial, the European Business Wallet shall perform cryptographic operations processing critical assets in accordance with the characteristics and design requirements for electronic identification means at assurance level substantial in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1502. rewording proposedProposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Annex, point 3(3)proposal, L; needs-legal-review
EBW-CER-010The wallet secure cryptographic applications and devices shall comply with the requirements for the characteristics and design of electronic identification means at assurance level substantial in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1502.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Annex, point 4(1)(g)proposal, L; needs-legal-review

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Machines and plants cannot use a phone; hosted or HSM custody is the default in practice.
  • IPCEI-AI: Agents sign on behalf of an organisation; custody decides who can stop them.
  • IDTA: Signed passports outlive the provider; key portability and exit matter.

Question for the session: Which custody models must be supported, and which must stay possible for exit?

T5 Identity across borders

Question
Which identifiers are primary (EUID, EBWOID, EU Company Certificate), which secondary (BPN, LEI, GLN, domain identifiers), and how does an EBW holder deal with partners outside the EU or without a business wallet?
Clusters
K06, K17
Decision
DEC-11: Primary and secondary identifiers, assurance and partners outside the EU; see also DEC-05
Sources are silent on
There is no official LEI credential outside the KERI-based vLEI; Catena-X issues BPNL only for some legal forms and has one issuing organisation.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-INT-095The attestation scheme for owner identification data shall state which attributes, in particular the unique identifier, may be disclosed separately and which only together with other attributes. rewording proposedProposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 8(6), read with Article 5(1)(b) and Article 8(5)proposal, D; needs-legal-review
EBW-FUN-044The provider of European Business Wallets shall request owner identification data from a provider of owner identification data on behalf of an economic operator established in a third country.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 18(4)proposal, L; needs-legal-review
EBW-INT-005European Business Wallet owner identification data shall contain at least the official name of the economic operator or public sector body as recorded in the register or official record, and its unique identifier.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 8(5)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Ecosystem identifiers (BPN) already run; the mapping to EUID and EBWOID decides whether they can be bridged.
  • IPCEI-AI: Cross-border agents need the identity and assurance level of the organisation, not of the person.
  • IDTA: Manufacturer and operator identifiers in passports must resolve to a legal entity.

Question for the session: Which identifier is the anchor, and which mappings must be standardised first?

T6 Trust and discovery

Question
Which trust lists, lists of trusted entities and ecosystem trust anchors does a relying party use, and how does it find the right one?
Clusters
K05, K14
Decision
DEC-04: Trust framework and discovery
Sources are silent on
How ecosystem trust anchors (Catena-X, Gaia-X) relate to the EU trusted lists; no source read links them. See the decision page.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-TRU-102The provider of European Business Wallets shall sign or seal the European Business Wallet unit attestation with a certificate issued under a certificate listed in the trusted list referred to in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2980.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Annex, point 2proposal, L
EBW-TRU-032The wallet unit shall authenticate and validate the wallet-relying party access certificate when interacting with a wallet-relying party.Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2982 (protocols and interfaces of the European Digital Identity Framework), Article 3(1)in force, L
EBW-TRU-034The wallet unit shall request issuance of person identification data or attestations only from parties with an authentic and valid wallet-relying party access certificate attesting them as a provider of person identification data or of the relevant type of attestation.Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2982 (protocols and interfaces of the European Digital Identity Framework), Article 4(2)in force, L

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Data spaces run their own trust anchors; relying parties need one way to combine them with EU lists.
  • IPCEI-AI: Agents verify counterparties automatically; discovery must work without a human.
  • IDTA: Who may sign a passport or an attestation of conformity is a trust question.

Question for the session: Which trust anchors must a verifier accept in a first cross-ecosystem scenario?

T7 Lifecycle and evidence

Question
How are validity, suspension and revocation handled for credentials, units and mandates, and what is logged and for whom?
Clusters
K07, K10
Decision
DEC-06: Credential lifecycle and revocation; see also DEC-09
Sources are silent on
The EBW texts give no status mechanism of their own for owner identification data, business attestations or authorisations.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-FUN-003The European Business Wallet shall enable the owner to securely issue, request, obtain, select, combine, store, delete, share and present electronic attestations of attributes.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 5(1)(a)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)
EBW-TRU-013The provider of European Business Wallets shall ensure that the validity of a wallet can be revoked on explicit owner request, on compromise, on cessation of owner activity, and where the provider is not on the list of notified providers.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 6(2)(f)proposal, L; needs-source-check: trigger list truncated in quote
EBW-TRU-106When the provider of European Business Wallets has revoked a wallet unit attestation, it shall make the validity status of that attestation publicly available and describe the location of that information in the attestation.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Annex, point 6(3)proposal, L; needs-legal-review

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Authority of staff and machines changes often; latency of revocation is an operating risk.
  • IPCEI-AI: Agent mandates must be revocable at once, with an audit trail.
  • IDTA: A passport must stay verifiable after a certificate or a company ceases to exist.

Question for the session: Which revocation latency is acceptable, and is suspension needed beside revocation?

T8 Protocols and delivery

Question
Which issuance, presentation, data space and delivery protocols must a business wallet implement, and how do they fit together?
Clusters
K08, K12
Decision
DEC-07: Protocols and interfaces
Sources are silent on
See the decision page for the protocols that no source requires.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-FUN-014The European Business Wallet shall support common protocols and interfaces that allow interaction with the wallet automatically without manual intervention or through direct user action.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 6(1)(d)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)
EBW-INT-002The European Business Wallet shall enable the owner to request and share owner identification data and electronic attestations of attributes in a secured way between European Business Wallets and European Digital Identity Wallets and with relying parties.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 5(1)(c)proposal, L; needs-source-check (quote not stored in file)
EBW-TRU-009The European Business Wallet shall support common protocols and interfaces for authenticating relying parties by implementing authentication mechanisms, where authentication is required.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 6(1)(g)proposal, L; needs-legal-review

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Connector protocols (DSP, DCP) and wallet protocols (OID4VCI, OID4VP) must meet at one point.
  • IPCEI-AI: Agent protocols (MCP, A2A) sit above them and need verifiable identity.
  • IDTA: Passport exchange runs over APIs; the credential protocols must not duplicate them.

Question for the session: Where do wallet protocols and data space protocols meet?

T9 Privacy and adoption

Question
How are minimisation, unlinkability and controller roles designed when the owner is an organisation and the users are people, and what makes the wallet accepted across ecosystems?
Clusters
K09, K15
Decision
DEC-08: Privacy and data protection
Sources are silent on
Whether the EUDI Wallet privacy rules apply to the business wallet is not stated in the EBW texts read.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-OPS-002On suspension, revocation or voluntary termination of its services, or removal from the list of notified providers, the provider of European Business Wallets shall notify owners and ensure the transfer or deletion of owner data according to the owner's instructions.Proposal for a Regulation on European Business Wallets, COM(2025) 838 final, Article 7(6)(f)proposal, L; needs-source-check
EBW-LEG-013The wallet provider shall design provider-held stores and registries so that personal data can be erased without undue delay where an erasure ground in Article 17(1) applies.Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Article 17(1)in force, D; needs-legal-review
EBW-CON-007Where a European Business Wallet owner or authorised user makes use of a qualified trust service forming part of the core functionalities, the resulting action shall have the same legal effect as if carried out in person, in paper form or by other compliant means.Council document ST 9684/26 (general approach note on European Business Wallets), Article 4, first paragraphproposal, L; needs-legal-review

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Employee data in logs and credentials meets works council and GDPR rules.
  • IPCEI-AI: Processing by AI must follow purpose limits; evidence must not leak personal data.
  • IDTA: Passports mix business secrets and personal data; disclosure must be selective.

Question for the session: What may an organisation see about its employees’ use of the wallet?

T10 Planes and evidence graph

Question
Where are policies decided and enforced (control plane), how do credentials, evidence and data move (data plane), and should evidence be expressed as one access-controlled linked-data graph under open-world semantics for policy, register, product passport and AI processes?
Clusters
K18, K01, K10, K16
Decision
DEC-12: Control plane, data plane and the verifiable evidence graph planned
Sources are silent on
No EU text read requires or forbids a linked-data graph, and none names ODRL, XACML or OPA for policies. The JSON-LD credential format is outside the legal list of CIR 2026/1731. CIR 2026/1778 (product passport registry) mentions ontologies and typed links to evidence but requires no RDF.

Key requirements

RequirementStatementSourceStatus
EBW-NFR-041The European Business Wallet provider shall log and timestamp all access and execution events and bind them to cryptographically verifiable proofs of authorisation suitable for audit and legal proceedings.Council document ST 9684/26 (general approach note on European Business Wallets), Annex, point 12(2)(c)proposal, L; needs-legal-review
EBW-TRU-059The provider of European Business Wallets shall ensure that all access and execution events are logged, timestamped and bound to cryptographically verifiable proofs of authorisation suitable for audit and legal proceedings.Council document ST 9684/26 (general approach note on European Business Wallets), Annex point 12(2)(c)proposal, L; needs-source-check
EBW-TRU-054Where the wallet or an agent acts for a legal person over A2A, the wallet provider shall bind the agent to the legal person by wallet credentials or equivalent evidence, because A2A leaves identity semantics to the protocol layer.Agent2Agent (A2A) Protocol Specification, Section 7 Authentication and Authorizationecosystem, D

What each group needs from the answer

  • Industry 4.0: Policies for data sharing (usage control) and evidence of supply chain facts need one model.
  • IPCEI-AI: Open-world linked data supports retrieval and reasoning; access control must follow the data.
  • IDTA: Passports as graphs of verifiable facts; the shell stays one possible carrier.

Question for the session: Is a shared evidence graph the common layer, or does each process keep its own format?
