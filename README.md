# European Business Wallet Requirements

A public, version-controlled requirements baseline for **European Business Wallets (EBW)** and the surrounding trust infrastructure.

Every requirement is meant to be **traceable** from formal sources (law, regulation, standards) through obligations and requirements to architecture building blocks, conformance checks and industry use cases.

> **Status: working draft.** The EBW proposal (COM(2025) 838, procedure 2025/0358(COD)) is still in the legislative process. Content may change and is **analysis, not legal advice**.

## Scope

- Functional, non-functional, interoperability, trust/assurance, certification, governance, legal/compliance, data-space, operational, domain-specific, business and transition requirements
- Industry-domain perspectives: Digital Product Passports (with and without the Asset Administration Shell), data spaces (DSP/DCP), automotive, pharma/GxP, industrial, agentic and physical AI
- Legal and compliance sources: EBW, eIDAS 2.0, GDPR, NIS2, CRA, Data Act, AI Act, ESPR/DPP and sector regulation
- Standards: W3C, OpenID, IETF, ISO/IEC, ETSI/CEN, IDTA, Eclipse, IDSA, Gaia-X, Catena-X
- Architecture alternatives organised by trust, control, data, governance and assurance planes
